# Simulation interpretation — detectability control

Each condition used 60 donors (15 cases), 120 nuclei per donor, and 30 generated datasets. Values in the supplement are mean±SD across datasets. These are idealized implementation checks, not biological models or a complete dimension/effect-size power surface.

A strong mean-preserving loop is recovered in 2-D but not when embedded in the evaluated 32-D nuisance setting. Therefore the implementation is not uniformly insensitive to loops, yet the empirical negative result cannot distinguish biological absence of shape signal from inadequate high-dimensional recovery. Persistence-image parameters in simulation are fitted at the generated-dataset level rather than within each classification fold, further limiting use to detectability checks.
